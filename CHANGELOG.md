# Changelog

All notable changes to Stridecore are listed here. The format follows Keep a Changelog.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Online Store 2.0 theme with JSON templates for every page type, including the password page and gift card page.
- Five color schemes, Shopify font pickers and shape settings (corner radius, offset shadows).
- Block-based product page with variant swatches, accelerated checkout, Shop Pay Installments messaging, pickup availability, unit prices, tax notes, complementary and related products, gift card recipient form, selling plans and app blocks.
- Cart drawer or cart page with discounts, order notes, upsell and a free shipping progress bar.
- Predictive search, filtering and sorting on collection and search pages, and quick add on product cards.
- Header with three-level menus, announcement ticker, country and language selectors, and the Shopify account component.
- 36 sections for the home page and other pages, including a Custom Liquid section and Custom Liquid blocks.
- Customer account pages, blog, article and 404 pages.
- Structured data for products, accessibility support (skip link, focus management, reduced motion) and responsive layouts.
