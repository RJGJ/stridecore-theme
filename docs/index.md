# Stridecore theme guide

Stridecore is a bold, high-contrast theme for sports apparel and streetwear stores. It is built for Online Store 2.0, so every page is made of sections you can add, remove and reorder in the theme editor.

> **Duplicate your theme before editing.** In Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes**, click **...** next to Stridecore and choose **Duplicate**. Work on the copy and keep the original as a backup. Shopify does not keep an undo history for theme edits made after you publish.

A full list of every setting is in the [settings reference](reference.md).

## Get started

1. Add the theme to your store, then open **Online Store > Themes > Customize**.
2. Choose the **Stridecore** preset if you are asked to pick one.
3. Set your logo in **Header**. Without a logo, the theme shows your store name.
4. Pick colors and fonts in **Theme settings** (see below).
5. Build your navigation in **Content > Menu**, then pick the menus in the header and footer sections.
6. Add products and collections, then arrange the home page sections.

## Colors and fonts

**Colors.** Open **Theme settings > Colors**. The theme includes five color schemes. Each scheme has a background, text color, accent color and a text color for use on the accent. You can edit them or add more.

- The **Default color scheme** is used for the page background.
- Almost every section has its own **Color scheme** setting, so you can mix dark and light bands on one page.
- Choose accent colors that contrast well with the text you place on them. Aim for at least 4.5:1 for small text.

**Fonts.** Open **Theme settings > Typography**. Pick a heading font, a body font and an accent font from the Shopify font library. The accent font is used for small labels, prices and tickers, and a monospace font suits it. Turn on **Use uppercase headings** for the bold streetwear look, or turn it off for sentence-case headings.

**Shape.** Under **Theme settings > Shape**, set the corner radius from 0 (sharp) upward, and choose whether buttons and cards show an offset shadow.

## Header, menus and footer

- **Announcement ticker:** a scrolling message bar above the header. Edit the messages in the **Announcement ticker** section.
- **Header:** set the logo and logo height, choose the main menu, and set the search placeholder. Menus with child links appear as dropdowns on desktop and as an accordion on mobile. Menus can be three levels deep.
- **Account:** the header shows the customer account link. With the new customer accounts, Shopify's sign-in component is used.
- **Footer:** add the footer menu, payment icons (turn on **Show payment icons** in Theme settings), social media links and an email signup form.
- **Social media:** add your profile links in **Theme settings > Social media**. Icons only appear for the networks you fill in.

## Home page

The home page is built from sections. Click **Add section** to see everything available, including:

| Section | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | Large headline with buttons and image tiles |
| Slideshow | Rotating banners (never auto-plays for visitors who prefer reduced motion) |
| Image banner | One image with text over it |
| Image with text | Image next to text |
| Featured products | A grid of products from a collection |
| Featured collection / Featured collections | Highlight one or more collections |
| Category tiles | Large tiles that link to collections |
| Big marquee, Ticker band | Scrolling text bands |
| Lookbook | Large editorial image next to a story, heading and button |
| Perks | Icon and text benefits (shipping, returns and so on) |
| Testimonials | Customer quotes |
| Multicolumn | Columns with images and text |
| Logo list | Press or brand logos |
| Video | YouTube, Vimeo or hosted video |
| Blog posts | Latest articles |
| Newsletter | Email signup |
| Collapsible content, FAQ | Question and answer lists |
| Rich text | Headings, text and buttons |
| Custom Liquid | Your own Liquid, HTML or app snippets |

Most sections let you set a **Color scheme** and top and bottom padding.

## Collections, filtering and search

- **Collection page:** choose products per page, card size and image ratio. You can show a promo tile in the grid, show the collection image and description, and add a sub-collection menu.
- **Filters and sorting:** filters come from the **Shopify Search & Discovery** app. Install it, then add the filters you want (availability, price, color, size and more). They appear on collection and search pages automatically.
- **Quick add:** the card button adds a product with a single option set. Products with several options open the product page.
- **Search:** the search icon opens a predictive search that shows products, collections, pages and articles as people type. The full search results page has filters and can include pages and articles. Set the placeholder text and popular searches in the **Search page** section.

## Product pages

The product page is block-based. In **Product information**, add, remove and reorder blocks: vendor, heading, price, description, variant picker, quantity, buy buttons, pickup availability, accordion, perks, share, text, Custom Liquid and app blocks.

- **Buy buttons** show **Add to cart** and, when you enable them in your payment settings, accelerated checkout buttons such as Shop Pay. Shop Pay Installments messaging appears when your store qualifies.
- **Variant picker** shows color swatches when your color option is linked to swatch values, and buttons for other options.
- **Media:** images, video and 3D models. Pick the gallery layout (stacked or thumbnails) and media ratio. Focal points you set on images are respected when the image is cropped.
- **Complementary and related products** are added with the **Complementary products** and **Related products** sections.
- **Unit prices** and **tax-included** notes show when they apply to the product and your markets.
- **Pickup availability** shows stock at your local pickup locations.
- **Gift card products** show the recipient form (email, name, message and send date).

## Cart and checkout

Under **Theme settings > Cart**, choose **Drawer** (slides in from the side) or **Page**. Set a **Free shipping threshold** to show a progress bar, or set it to 0 to hide it. The cart supports order notes, discounts, selling plans, unit prices and an upsell section. Checkout is hosted by Shopify.

## Customer accounts

Login, registration, order history, addresses, password reset and account activation pages are all styled by the theme. If you use the new customer accounts, Shopify hosts those pages instead.

## Markets, languages and currencies

If your store sells in more than one country or language, selectors appear in the header and footer. You can turn each off in the section settings. Set them up in **Settings > Markets** and **Settings > Languages**. The theme ships with English text; use Shopify's translate and adapt app or edit the locale files to add other languages.

## Gift cards and local pickup

- **Gift cards:** gift card products and the gift card page (with QR code and Apple Wallet link) are included.
- **Local pickup:** turn on local pickup in **Settings > Shipping and delivery**. The product page shows pickup availability automatically.

## Accessibility

The theme is built to WCAG 2.1 AA: a skip link, visible keyboard focus, labelled form fields, dialogs that trap and restore focus, and reduced-motion support for the ticker, slideshow and video. Automated checks cover every page type. Please still check your own content: write descriptive image alt text and keep text and background colors contrasting.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change the fonts to a custom font?** Pick any font from the Shopify font library in **Theme settings > Typography**.

**Why doesn't my filter show?** Filters come from the Search & Discovery app. Install it and add filters.

**How do I change the Free shipping message?** Set the threshold in **Theme settings > Cart**. The message text is part of the cart translations (**Online Store > Themes > Edit default theme content**).

**Where do I put app embeds?** Use the **App embeds** tab in the theme editor, or add an app block to supported sections.

**How do I update the theme?** Duplicate your live theme, add the new version from the Theme Store, copy over your settings if needed, and publish when you are happy.
